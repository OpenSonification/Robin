Explain the map

Robin is an 11 by 11 grid from minus 5 to 5 on both axes. Moving up raises the pitch, and moving left or right moves the sound between your headphones. With VoiceOver on, start Robin audio once, then touch or swipe to a cell to hear its position tone. An empty cell plays the position tone on its own; plotted shapes add their sounds on top. Double-tap the focused cell to plot the selected shape.

Touch controls

These controls are for touchscreen phones and tablets. There are no keyboard or directional-arrow controls in this version.

  1. Activate Start Robin audio above the map once.
  2. Choose the shape you want to plot.
  3. With VoiceOver on, drag one finger over the map or swipe between cells. Robin plays every cell as soon as VoiceOver focuses it, including an empty cell.
  4. Double-tap the focused cell to plot the selected shape.

Focused cell actions

These browser-accessible buttons provide the delete and playback actions for the cell most recently focused or selected.

How to use it

Arrow keys
Move around the grid from anywhere on the page.
S C T D
Select square, circle, triangle, or diamond.
Shift
Plot the selected shape in the focused cell.
Shift + arrows
Plot the selected shape while moving.
Backspace
Remove the most recently plotted point in the cell.
1
Play the current row from left to right.
2
Play the current column from bottom to top.
3
Play only plotted shapes, one column at a time.
4
Play only plotted shapes, one row at a time.
Space
Turn the screen black or restore the graphics.
Tab
Move between the controls on the left.
Enter / Space
Use the button that currently has focus.

Headphones make the left-to-right movement easiest to hear.
